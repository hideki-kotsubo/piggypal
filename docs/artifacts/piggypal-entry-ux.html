Two calls worth confirming

A

App shell: single scroll, or bottom tabs?

Frames above assume one continuous Home screen — entry, budgets, trend, and recent all stacked, with only Settings and "see all transactions" as drill-ins. The alternative is a conventional bottom tab bar (Home / Transactions / Budgets / Settings). Single-scroll is more minimal and keeps entry literally one thumb-reach from anything else on screen; tabs are more familiar and scale better once budgets or transactions need their own dedicated screen space.

B

Keypad visible by default, or reveal on tap?

Frame 1 shows the entry zone collapsed to a single placeholder line until tapped (frame 2), keeping budgets and recent activity visible by default. The alternative is entry-first: the amount pad and chips always expanded, pushing budget/trend further down. That trades glanceability for shaving the last bit of friction off the <3s target — worth deciding which matters more for the home screen's first impression.
