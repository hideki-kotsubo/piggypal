Where you spent it, not just what and how much

Sketching merchant as a new nullable field on transactions — extracted by the Tier 2 AI parser ("at Costco"), editable manually, and the thing a search/filter screen would key off of. Nothing here is built or decided; this is the visual half of the brainstorm, alongside the backlog entry.

What this brainstorm doesn't resolve

A

Structured field, or fold into Note?

The cheap version costs nothing: "at Costco" just lands in the existing free-text Note, searchable but not filterable/countable. The structured version shown here (new merchant column, AI schema addition, two new UI surfaces) is what frame 3's filter chips and future spend-by-merchant reporting actually require. Recommend starting cheap (Note-only) and promoting to a structured field only once search/filter is itself prioritized — but that's the call this artifact exists to surface, not a decision made here.

B

Does Tier 1 (offline, rule-based) attempt merchant at all?

Category keyword-matching is a closed, known vocabulary (docs/04) — merchant names are open-ended proper nouns, a much fuzzier extraction problem. Frame 1 assumes Tier 2 (online, Claude) only, with Tier 1 always leaving Location blank rather than guessing — consistent with "ambiguity falls into the inbox, never a wrong guess." Worth confirming that split explicitly if this gets built, same as docs/04's existing Tier 1/Tier 2 boundary for category and account.
