What this does and doesn't fix

13 top-level rows instead of 18 is real, but you still have 12 distinct banks — grouping can't shrink that number, only organize it. What it actually buys you: rows are no longer uniform pills you have to fully read to tell apart, the "which card at this bank" detail is hidden until you need it, and it reuses a pattern you already have (docs/12's institution grouping) instead of inventing a second one. If the real goal is fewer things to scan rather than better-organized things to scan, the recency-capped "top 6 + more" approach from earlier would cut harder, just less legibly connected to how you actually think about your banks.
