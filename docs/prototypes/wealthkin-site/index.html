Why it exists

Built for lives that span countries.

Rent in reais. Groceries in dollars. A transit card in yen from last month's trip. Most money apps assume your whole life happens in one currency, one country, one language. Yours doesn't.

Wealthkin records every entry in its original currency, forever. Reports convert only when you ask, at the rate you choose — so history stays true even when exchange rates don't.

And it listens the way your household actually talks. One person says "café, cinco reais", another says "groceries, forty-two dollars", a third says "tacos, ochenta pesos" — all land in the same shared ledger, correctly categorized, tagged with who said it.

What's in the book

A proper ledger, not another dashboard.

When, where, what, to whom, how much, from which account — every entry carries the full story, and every report can filter on it.

Every currency is first-class

Entries keep their original currency for good. Mix BRL, CAD, JPY, and anything else in one ledger; pick the report currency at report time, not entry time.

no-login

No account until you want one

Everything works locally with zero sign-up: your entries live on your device. Create an identity only when you want sync, family sharing, or the voice AI.

kin

Whole household, own entries

Everyone records their own way — voice, text, quick tap — into a shared household book. Each entry shows who added it. Personal wallets stay personal.

Σ filter

Reports that answer real questions

"How much went to family in Brazil this year?" "What did the Japan trip cost in CAD?" Filter by person, tag, payee, account, currency, place, or period.

cash + card

Cash and transit cards count too

Track wallets, cards, and top-up cards like Compass or Suica. Loading a card is a transfer, not an expense — so nothing gets counted twice.

offline-first

Works on the plane

Add entries with no signal; they sync when you're back. Built offline-first from the ground up — not a cached copy of a website.
