What's new

Every Flowtab release, newest first.

0.14.0

Oct 28, 2026
  • A tidier Settings. Settings is now a short page of groups (your money, sync and household, preferences, app), each opening its own page, with your sync status at the top.
  • A page for each account. Tap an account to see its balance, money in and out this month, scheduled payments coming up, and its latest entries. Edit it from there.
  • Accounts are easier to find. Accounts are grouped in a card per bank, with search once you have a lot of them, totals for each currency, and filters per person in a household.
  • Categories at a glance. Switch between Expense and Income, see what each category spent this month, and how far along its budget is. Subcategories hang clearly under their group.
  • Editing slides up from the bottom. Accounts and categories now open in a panel instead of inside the list, so it's always clear what you're editing.
  • Bring back archived categories and accounts. Archived ones now have a Restore button.

0.13.0

Oct 27, 2026
  • "Only me" entries. In a household, tick Only me on an entry you logged and it syncs only to your own devices. Nobody else in the household sees it, and you can switch it back to shared any time.
  • A PIN for each person in the household. Set it in Settings → Household. A new device can only sign in as you, and get your Only me entries, once your PIN is entered on it.
  • Forgot your PIN? Change it on another device that's already you, or ask someone in the household to reset it from theirs. Nothing is lost, and you'll see who reset it and on which device. The account owner can also get a reset code by email.

Oct 16, 2026
  • "Dollars" means dollars. Saying "15 dollars" or typing "$15" no longer falls back to your account's currency (like reais). Flowtab picks a dollar: your account's own, the last one you used, or the new Dollars ($) mean setting (Canadian or US). Say "canadian dollars" or "US dollars" to be exact.

0.6.0

Oct 17, 2026

Flowtab now speaks your language.

  • Português (Brasil), Español and Français, alongside English. The app follows your device's language, or pick one in Settings → Language. Spain and Latin America, and France and Canada, each get their own wording where it differs.
  • Your starter categories and accounts follow the language too: Groceries becomes Mercado, Supermercado or Épicerie. Devices on the same account can each use a different language. Categories you've renamed or created stay exactly as you wrote them.
  • Dates and amounts are written the way your region writes them.
  • Voice language in Settings: speak in a different language from the app's, for example the app in English while you say "quarenta e cinco no mercado".
  • Typing or saying what you spent now understands Spanish and French too, alongside English and Portuguese ("treinta y cinco en el súper", "trente-cinq euros de courses hier").
  • Voice entry on Android now uses the phone's own speech recognition.
  • The sign-in email and the iPhone permission prompts are in your language.

0.3.0

Oct 9, 2026

The first public version of Flowtab: a simple, private way to keep track of what you spend.

  • Type it or say it: "coffee 4.25" or "almoço 32", in English or Portuguese, and Flowtab fills in the amount, category, account and date for you to confirm.
  • Works offline: everything is stored on your device first, so it's fast and works without a connection.
  • Every currency side by side: log each expense in the currency you paid in. Totals are kept per currency, never converted.
  • Accounts and categories your way: group accounts by bank, and organise categories into groups.
  • Budgets and insights: monthly budgets per category, and a trend chart of how this month compares.
  • Find anything: search your expenses and filter by category, account, place or date.
  • Split a purchase across two or more accounts.
  • Scheduled payments: rent, bills and installment plans show up when they're due, and count towards your budgets ahead of time.
  • Share with your household: pair two phones with a QR code to combine your expenses.
  • Sign in with your email to keep your data in sync across your devices.
  • Install it on your phone or computer, straight from the browser.
