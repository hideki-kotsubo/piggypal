scheduled payments

Rent, bills and installments, before they happen

Set up a payment once and Flowtab shows it when it's due, so your budget already knows what's coming.

  • ✓Recurring payments every week, month or year: rent, tuition, subscriptions.
  • ✓Installment plans (parcelado): a total split over any number of payments, counted down as you go.
  • ✓One tap when it's paid, or skip it. It becomes a normal expense in your list.
  • ✓Budgets show what's committed: spent so far, plus what's already scheduled this month.

what's in it today

Small on purpose. Built for a real household.

Everything here works now, in the app you can open today.

PT · EN

Bilingual, for real

Say or type an expense in Portuguese or English. The parser runs on your device, so it works offline too.

¤ side by side

Every currency, never blended

CAD, BRL, USD, whatever you paid in. Totals are kept per currency, never silently converted into one number.

offline-first

Your data, on your device

Every entry is saved on your device first. The app never waits on a network to show you your own data.

budgets

Budgets and trends

A monthly budget per category, and a trend chart of how this month compares with the ones before.

split

Split a purchase

Paid with two cards? Split one purchase across accounts and it still counts once, in one category.

search

Find anything

Search every expense, and filter by category, account, place or date, with per-currency totals.

household

Share with your household

Pair two phones with a QR code to combine your expenses, and see who paid for what.

opt-in sync

Sync when you want it

No account needed to start. Sign in with your email only when you want your data on all your devices.

install
