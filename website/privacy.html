Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

Flowtab keeps your budget on your own device by default, and we never sell your data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Flowtab is an independent budgeting app developed in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. It is operated by Hideki Kotsubo, a sole proprietor in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, who is responsible for your information under this policy ("Flowtab", "we", "us"). This policy applies to the Flowtab apps for iOS and Android, the web app at app.myflowtab.com, the website at myflowtab.com, and the servers behind them (together, the "Service").

3. How we use information

We use your information only to run Flowtab for you. Specifically, to:

  • Sign you in by emailing you a one-time link or code.
  • Sync your budget between your devices and with household members you invite.
  • Keep the Service secure, such as limiting repeated sign-in attempts and investigating abuse.
  • Answer your support requests.
  • Send essential messages about your account or important changes to this policy. We do not send marketing email.
  • Meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles of you. We do not use your budget data to train artificial intelligence models.

  • People you choose. If you create or join a household, its members see the shared budget data and your profile name. If you pair two devices directly, data moves between those devices. Our pairing relay only helps the two devices find each other; it does not see or store your budget data.

5. Storage, security and retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Synced data stays on our servers until you delete your account.

Security. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS). We use passwordless sign-in, so there is no password to steal. Sign-in links, codes, and session tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. In the mobile apps, your session is kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). Access to our servers is limited to the people who operate Flowtab. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as the law requires.

6. Your rights and choices

You can see, export, correct, or delete your data at any time, in most cases directly in the app.

  • Use Flowtab without an account. Sync and sign-in are optional.
  • See and correct. Everything you enter can be viewed and edited in the app.
  • Export. Download your transactions as a CSV file from the app.
  • Delete data on a device. Use the option in Settings to erase all data on that device, or uninstall the app.
  • Delete your account. In the app, go to Settings, then Delete account. This deletes your account and all synced data from our servers straight away, for everyone in your household, so only the person who created the account can do it. You can also email us at privacy@myflowtab.com from the address you sign in with, and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm when it is done. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. Data already on your devices stays there until you erase it.
  • Permissions. Turn microphone or camera access off at any time in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as Canada's PIPEDA and British Columbia's PIPA, Brazil's LGPD, the EU and UK GDPR, or California's CCPA. These can include the right to know what we hold, to receive a copy, to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email us; we will reply within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or Brazil's ANPD.

Legal bases (GDPR and LGPD). We process your data to provide the Service you ask for (contract), to keep it secure (legitimate interest), with your consent for device permissions, and to meet legal obligations.

7. Children

Flowtab is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age for consent in your country. We do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our servers and providers may be located outside your country, including in Canada and the United States. When we transfer your information, we protect it as this policy describes and use safeguards required by law, such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any update here and change the effective date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

10. Contact us

For questions, requests, or complaints about privacy, email privacy@myflowtab.com. We reply within 30 days.
