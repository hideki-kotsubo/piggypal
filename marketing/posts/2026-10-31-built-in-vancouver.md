Hi 👋 Flowtab is a one-person app, built in Vancouver.

I'm Brazilian, and I spend in Reais, Canadian dollars, US dollars and whatever the next trip needs. Every budget app I tried either wanted me to link my bank, converted everything into one made-up total, or took too long to log a coffee. So I built the one I wanted: type or say what you spent, in your language, and it's on the tab.

It's free, works offline and doesn't need an account. Follow along: I'll post what I'm building, what breaks and what's next.
