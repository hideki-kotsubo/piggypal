Flowtab got it wrong? Here's how to fix an entry in seconds. 🛠️

1. Read the preview. Before anything is saved, Flowtab shows what it understood: amount, category, when and account.
2. Wrong currency or account? Tap it: both open a list. Pick the right one and Save.
3. Wrong category or date? Tap Edit. Your text comes back, so add a word ("groceries", "yesterday") and press Enter.
4. Saved too fast? Tap Undo right away, or tap the entry in your list to open it and change anything.
