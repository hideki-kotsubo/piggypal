How does Flowtab turn "uber 18 yesterday" into a saved expense? 🔍

No AI server, no upload. Your phone reads it, word by word:
• 18 → the amount, in the currency you usually spend in ("R$ 18" or "18 USD" if you say so)
• uber → Rideshare, from a list of everyday words for each of the seven languages
• yesterday → the date
