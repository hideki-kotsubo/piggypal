## en
headline: New in Flowtab 0.7.0
bullets:
- "15 dollars" now means dollars, Canadian or US: your pick
- Change the currency and account right before saving
- Switch the voice language from Home: EN, PT, ES, FR
caption:
New in Flowtab 0.7.0 💵

• "Dollars" means dollars. Saying "15 dollars" or typing "$15" no longer falls back to your account's currency. Flowtab picks the dollar you use, or set Canadian or US in Settings. Say "US dollars" to be exact.
• Change the currency and account before saving: tap them in the "Understood" panel.
• Voice language from Home: tap the little chip next to the mic. Switch even while it's listening.
• CA$ and US$: once you use both, amounts say which dollar they are.

Novidades do Flowtab 0.7.0 💵

• CA$ e US$: quando você usa os dois, cada valor mostra qual dólar é.
